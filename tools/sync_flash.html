The big square flashes white for the first 100 ms of every UTC second.
The bottom bar shows (UTC seconds mod 4096) in binary, most significant bit on the left, so any single camera frame can be decoded to an absolute second.
The strip under the square cycles through 10 cells, each 100 ms, to read sub-second phase.
Sync the PCs with NTP first. Film both screens at the same time; any frame offset between their flashes is the residual clock error.
